About This App

To-Do List

Welcome to the Motivational To-Do App!

This app helps you stay productive and motivated every day:

  • ✅ Daily Reset: Your to-do list resets every day, so you can build habits and track daily progress.
  • 📝 Notes: Add notes or details to each task for better context.
  • 💡 Motivational Quotes: Get a new motivational quote every time you visit.
  • 📤 Export: Download your to-do list as an Excel file for record-keeping or sharing.
  • 🔔 Email Reminders: (Optional) Get motivational reminders to complete your tasks.

Made with using Flask, Vercel, and a lot of motivation!
